This is a test of the AR before the real scenes are built. A supply crate falls from ceiling height and lands on the floor in front of you.

  • 1Stand where you have about two metres of clear floor ahead of you.
  • 2Tap start, then allow the camera — and on an iPhone, allow motion as well.
  • 3Hold the phone up and look down at the floor ahead of you.

Needs camera access. Nothing is recorded or uploaded — the video never leaves your phone.
